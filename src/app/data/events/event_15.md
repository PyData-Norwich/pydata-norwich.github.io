## Details

Join us for an evening of Python/Data Science related talks and discussion.

This time we'll have three members of Aviva's Risk Analytics team give insights into Data Science and Data Analytics within Aviva. The event is very informal with plenty of room for questions and all skill levels are welcome.

We'll also have pizza and drinks as usual and afterwards we'll take a trip to the local pub for anyone that wants to come along.

</section>

## Presentations

### Felipe Valencia-Clavjo, Robbie Southall and Yogi Bear  *(Aviva)*  - **Representing Aviva's Risk Analytics team (also known as RAID)**

We're part of the team building data tools to support second-line risk operations. What's the second line risk function? Well, come and find out. This is a rare chance to have a sneak peek behind the scenes at the group level of Aviva's in-house analytic tool builds, which enable staff to take risks more safely.
