## Details

Join us at PyData Norwich for something a little different this month. If your interested in the thought processes that go in to starting data-related companies, services, products etc. then this one is for you.

Whether you are just starting out or an experienced Python/Data professional then Yogi will be keen to get your thoughts and comments. This is a very informal event with plenty of room for discussion, equally if you just want to come along and listen then you are more than welcome.

We'll also have pizza and drinks as usual and afterwards we'll take a trip to the local pub for anyone that wants to come along.

</section>

## Presentation

### Yogi Louise Bear *(Aviva/YLB tech)*  - **Ever wished you could be a Dragon?**

Don't worry, I'm not asking for investment! As some of you know, I'm an AI professional, and for some time I have been exploring entrepreneurship. I have a few data product/service ideas and would appreciate both your technical expertise and perspectives on the opportunities I've identified.

This evening I'll introduce Large Bear and present four (maybe five, as I had a new idea this weekend too) proposals that need objective, critical, and constructive feedback.
Maybe you're stressed and need a safe space to let it out, or you're also thinking about setting up independently, or you just fancy hearing something a little different.

Please join us for a data ideation evening.
