# PyData Norwich - September Meetup

## Date

2026-09-30

<section markdown="1" class="event-detail">

## Details

Join us for an evening of Python/Data Science related talks and discussion (Note the change of venue for regulars!)

This is a very informal event where we will have a couple of interesting presentations (see details below) with plenty of room for questions. All skill levels are welcome and actively encouraged!

Afterwards we'll take a trip to the local pub for anyone that wants to come along

</section>

## Presentations

### Hugh Evans *(Aiven)* - **Mapping the PyData Community with Python and Web Scraping**

In this talk I'll be walking through my project building maps of the PyData community using data scraped from Meetup. Featuring insights into the PyData community, geo-encoding, map making with Folium, and a call to support your local PyData group.

### Sam Greenwood *(Independent consultant)* - **10 years of Data Engineering with Python**

Exploring Data Engineering work and its evolving relationship with the Python data world.
